# Voice lines

Lines a game passes to `kid.say()` (or Cru says itself). Short, warm, specific, one
idea. `{x}` = the thing (word, letter, number, colour). Rotate; never the same line
twice in a row. Under 4: ≤ 8 words where possible.

## Asking (one short question, then wait)

- 12–24 m: "Look! What's this?" · "Tap the door!" · "Who's hiding?"
- 2–3: "Where's the {x}?" · "Can you find {x}?" · "Show me {x}!"
- 3–4: "Which one is {x}?" · "How many ducks? Let's count!" · "Find the same one!"
- 4–5: "Which one starts with {sound}?" · "Find little {x}." · "What comes next?"
- 5–7: "Let's build {word}. What sound comes first?" · "Can you count on from {n}?"

## Celebrating a right answer

Labelling (12 m–3 y): "{X}! Yes!" · "Ball! Bouncy ball!" · "Moo! It's the cow!" ·
"You found it! {X}!" · "Yay, {x}!"

Specific (3–5): "Yes, that's {x}!" · "You found the {x}!" · "Three! One, two, three!" ·
"That's it — {letter} says {sound}!" · "Well spotted, {x}!" · "Right! The circle is round!"

Process / effort (4–7): "You listened really carefully!" · "You counted every one!" ·
"You looked at all of them — great looking!" · "You sounded it out: c-a-t, cat!" ·
"You remembered where it was!" · "You kept trying and you got it!" ·
"Clever thinking — you counted on!"

After help (any age, smaller): "You found it!" · "There it is — {x}!" · "Now you know {x}!"

## A miss (never "wrong", "no", "oops" for under-4s)

- 2–3: "That's the {other}! Where's {x}?" · "Ooh, that's {other}. Look — here's {x}!"
- 3–4: "Good try! Look for {x}." · "Nearly! Let's look again." · "Let's count together!"
- 4–5: "Hmm, listen: {sound}… {word}." · "Good try! Which one is {x}?"
- 5–7: "Try again!" · "Have another go — count them one by one." · "Listen to each sound."
- Showing: "Here it is! Tap it!" · "It's this one — {x}!"

## Finishing

- "You found them all! Bye-bye, animals!" · "You counted all the ducks!" ·
  "You know the sounds s, a and t!" · "You built five words — great sounding out!" ·
  "That was fun! Want to play again, or try something new?" (Cru says the last one.)

## Fun facts (one at a time, true, with a picture)

- "A cow's tummy has four parts to help it eat grass!" (not "four stomachs": one
  stomach, four compartments — say it the true way)
- "An octopus has eight arms!" · "Giraffes have very long necks to reach leaves!" ·
  "Bees make honey from flowers!" · "The sun is a star!" · "Penguins are birds that
  swim but can't fly!" · "Elephants use their trunks like a hand and a nose!"
  Check every fact before using it; avoid anything about predators eating, death,
  danger, or scary weather.

## Breaks (Cru, when the parent's rules or time say so)

"Time for a wiggle break! Can you jump like a frog?" · "Let's stretch up high like a
giraffe!" · "Time for a drink of water — then we can play again."

## Never

"Wrong!" · "No!" · "Hurry up!" · "You lost" · "Game over" · "Don't you want to
play?" · "I'll be sad if you stop" · "Good girl/boy" · "You're so smart" ·
anything about the child's appearance, address, school or family details.
