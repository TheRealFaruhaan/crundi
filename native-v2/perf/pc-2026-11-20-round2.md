## The eight-pane runs: an open shell comes back into the layout

In runs e and f the planning sweep was p95 8.0–8.2. The driver's pane list
before the sweep shows seven panes, but the profile (profile-planning-8panes-shell.txt)
paints `TermScreen` and `TermInput` inside the window and the renders count
term-screen 64–68 in the sweep: after planning's `measure-layout` replaces
the mosaic, `crundi_workspace::terms::sync` places the still-open shell
again (as the web shows open terminals). So those sweeps had eight panes,
one of them a terminal with 6000 lines of scrollback under the wheel. That
is F46's cost (the terminal under a sweep: term-stream+scroll 8.1–8.3), not
a leak. The harness now records the panes the sweep really had
(`report['planning+stream+scroll'].panes`).

## Where the time goes (gdb samples of the UI thread, 400 at ~12 ms)

Seven panes: 80 busy samples, spread through GPUI's frame: paint 26, render
19, layout 16, and no crundi function above 5. The workbench re-renders on
every frame (a child's notify dirties its ancestors) and lays out its grid;
the panes are reused from cache.

Eight panes with the shell: 73 busy samples, layout 20 (from 16), and the
terminal's screen painted and laid out under the wheel.

The profile showed the Kanban and the map reloaded from the server during
the sweep (`kanban::load`, `mindmap::load_from` from `on_batch`, ~11% of the
busy samples). A breakpoint counter with no pauses (`perf/gdb-count-loads.py`,
loads-during-sweep.txt) saw one reload of each in 20 s. The sampler's pauses
cause most of them; one reload in a sweep is not a cost worth chasing.

## Profiling with the sampler in a given scenario

`PROFILE_AT=planning` runs the sampler in planning+stream+scroll (after its
numbers are taken) instead of after scroll-chat:

    CRUNDI_CONFIG_DIR=$(mktemp -d …) MEASURE_REGIONS=0 PROFILE=native-v2/perf/gdb-sample.py \
      PROFILE_AT=planning SAMPLES=400 MEASURE_SERVER=http://localhost:4884 \
      node native-v2/tools/measure-pc.mjs /tmp/ptr-v2.sh /tmp/v2-prof

(`/tmp/ptr-v2.sh` execs `perf/ptracer-exec.py`; the binary comes from
`NATIVE_BIN` or the default release path, not from its arguments.)

On this PC the worktree needs `npm ci` first (the test server needs the
root's node_modules).
